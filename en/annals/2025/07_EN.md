# July 2025

## Mistral Large 2: Europe's Flagship 123B Update

**2025-07-24** — Mistral AI released **Mistral Large 2**, with 123B total parameters, supporting a 128K context window, and claiming performance on par with Llama 3.1 405B across multiple benchmarks. The model was released with both open weights and API access. [^1]

Mistral Large 2 continued Mistral's characteristic "efficiency-first" approach — not making the largest model, but making the model with the highest performance per parameter. At the time, a 123B parameter count was in the upper tier of the open-source/open-weight ecosystem, but its real selling point was multilingual capability (especially French, German, Spanish, and other European languages) and code generation quality. Mistral claimed that Large 2 matched or surpassed Llama 3.1 405B on coding and multilingual tasks, while requiring only a fraction of the inference cost.

This strategy was not surprising at the time. During the first half of 2025, the open-source ecosystem had evolved into a two-pole structure: Llama (Meta) and Mistral (France). Llama 3.1 405B dominated through sheer parameter scale, while Mistral differentiated through MoE architecture and more compact parameter counts. The release of Mistral Large 2 further confirmed Europe's presence in the open-source large language model race.
