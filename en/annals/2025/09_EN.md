# September 2025

## Anthropic Designates China as a Restricted Region

**2025-09** — Anthropic updated its terms of service, explicitly designating China as a restricted region. Enterprises with more than 50% Chinese ownership were prohibited from using Claude. The stated rationale was "U.S. national security policy — preventing advanced AI capabilities from being used for military and intelligence purposes." [^1]

This terms change did not attract widespread attention at the time, but its impact gradually became apparent months later. In February 2026, Anthropic accused three Chinese AI companies (DeepSeek, Moonshot AI, MiniMax) of conducting "industrial-scale model distillation attacks" on Claude through approximately 24,000 fake accounts — a timeline of allegations built upon the September 2025 restriction clause as its premise. [^1]

According to a March 2026 analysis article by AtomGit (an open-source community under CSDN), the causal chain ran: Anthropic restricts China in September 2025 → distillation attack exposed in February 2026 → risk control escalation → the "March 18 Incident" of March 2026 — and the starting point of this chain was precisely this terms update. [^1]

> ⚠ **Caveat**: The exact update date is unclear. Annals/2026/03.md cites an AtomGit article from 2026-03-29 stating "September 2025," but Anthropic's official blog posts do not mention the specific date of the terms update.
