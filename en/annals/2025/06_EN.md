# June 2025

## The "Succession Dispute of Linear Transformers": Peng Bo vs. Songlin Yang

> A dispute over who was the rightful heir to linear attention ended with a complete falling-out between the two parties and a collective cooling of enthusiasm for the Linear Transformer family.

### Background: The Hundred Schools of linear attention

Since the Transformer architecture dominated sequence modeling in 2017 with O(n²) complexity Softmax attention, "linear attention" — achieving approximate results with O(n) complexity — had remained the most active direction among alternative approaches. Around 2023, multiple linear attention architectures emerged simultaneously, forming a landscape of competing schools:

- **RWKV** (Peng Bo, GitHub: BlinkDL): Fuses RNN temporal decay with attention mechanisms, iterating from its first generation to the seventh (RWKV-7); its 2023 paper has been cited over a thousand times. Peng Bo is an independent developer with an uncompromising style. [^3]
- **Gated Linear Attention (GLA) / DeltaNet** (Songlin Yang, Zhihu: sonta): Gated linear attention with a focus on hardware-efficient implementation. Songlin Yang also maintains the open-source library **Flash Linear Attention (FLA)**, which provides unified high-performance operator implementations for various schools of linear attention. [^4]
- **RetNet** (Microsoft / Tsinghua University): A "two sides of one coin" architecture supporting both parallel training and serial inference.
- **Mamba** (Carnegie Mellon University): A non-attention approach based on state space models (SSMs), known for its data-dependent selective mechanism.
- **Su Jianlin** (Science Space blogger, known in the community as "Su Shen"): An independent NLP researcher who had prior disagreements with Peng Bo over a VAE-related matter.

FLA played a special role in this landscape: it did not create new architectures but provided unified Triton operator implementations for all schools, enabling researchers to conduct fair comparisons. Third-party implementations of RWKV-6 and RWKV-7 were also maintained by FLA. However, RWKV's original author Peng Bo's "magic initialization" was extremely difficult to align, and despite FLA's multiple attempts at fixes, it never received Peng Bo's "official certification."

### Course of events

**Early June 2025** — The benchmark **Zoology** published comparison results across linear attention models, in which **RWKV-7's scores were significantly lower than expected**. The RWKV-7 implementation used by Zoology came from the FLA library. Songlin Yang cited these results on X (Twitter) to promote his own DeltaNet.

**Peng Bo's response** — Peng Bo publicly replied under the tweet, suggesting the use of RWKV-LM's official implementation rather than the FLA version, implying that the third-party implementation was flawed. Songlin Yang perceived this as a public humiliation: FLA had been maintaining the RWKV implementation as a volunteer effort, and when Peng Bo found issues, instead of communicating privately (filing an issue or PR), he chose to make snide remarks in public.

**Escalation** — Songlin Yang, furious in FLA's 500-member WeChat group, threatened to delete all RWKV-related code from FLA and pinned the message "Trying to disgust people, huh?" for several days. Su Jianlin also brought up the old RWKV-4 incident in the group.

Peng Bo felt backstabbed — just days earlier, the two had been cordially discussing RWKV-8's DeepEmbed — and published a long post on Zhihu expressing his anger, listing specific errors in the FLA implementation (State Size calculation errors, omission of the Shortconv trick, etc.), and comparing himself to the *Zhuangzi* parable of the phoenix and the owl.

**Songlin Yang's response** — Published a Zhihu post titled *The Farmer and the Snake*, stating that FLA had written RWKV implementations out of goodwill, "better than RWKV-LM and loved by the RWKV open-source community (except Bo)." He pointed out that Peng Bo had a pattern of dismissing all third-party RWKV implementations — and even official implementations — as "buggy implementations" whenever results were unsatisfactory, calling this "bullying, not research."

**Su Jianlin enters the fray** — Published *Adding Some Entertainment to the Jianghu*, acknowledging his long-standing grudge against Peng Bo over a VAE theoretical dispute, stating he "does not like, and even resents" him, but emphasized "people are people, techniques are techniques," and was not an FLA partisan.

**Peng Bo's counterattack** — Directly compared Songlin Yang's DeltaNet against RWKV-7 on fair terms: simply replacing the DeltaNet kernel with the RWKV-7 kernel — only a few lines of code — improved performance, thereby demonstrating RWKV's theoretical inclusiveness.

**Final falling-out** — Songlin Yang announced on X that FLA would **no longer maintain RWKV-related code**, listing reasons including: Peng Bo's accusation that FLA deliberately lowered RWKV scores, involvement of sockpuppet accounts, a history of attacking others (referring to the RWKV-5 and RetNet similarity controversy), and "belittling contributors, spreading misinformation, and bullying dissenters." Peng Bo counter-accused Songlin Yang of being "mad" and used the S-word to describe RWKV. **Both parties blocked each other.**

### Aftermath and assessment

The incident received widespread attention on Zhihu (Pinski's "Cyber Historian" record post received 431 upvotes; Gunter Wand's Jin Yong-style chronicle *The Chronicles of Linear Attention* received 862 upvotes). [^1][^2] The community split into two camps: Peng Bo's supporters argued that academic benchmark alignment was a matter of principle, while Songlin Yang's supporters argued that Peng Bo's communication style had destroyed the foundation of open-source collaboration.

On a technical level, this dispute exposed a structural dilemma in the linear attention field: the subtle differences across architectures (initialization, kernel implementation, trick usage) made fair comparison extremely difficult, and the gap between third-party and official implementations further amplified this problem. Subsequently, **academic enthusiasm for linear attention architectures gradually cooled**, with research focus shifting to state space models (the Mamba family) and sparse attention mechanisms. The Linear Transformer family — RWKV, GLA, DeltaNet, RetNet — each continued to make progress, but none achieved a comprehensive replacement of Softmax attention, and the field **temporarily faded from the spotlight.**
