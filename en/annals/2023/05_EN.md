# May 2023

## SoVITS-SVC, the "AI Stefanie Sun" phenomenon, and the open-source voice synthesis wave

> A singing voice conversion tool that let "AI Stefanie Sun" sing across the entire Mandopop catalogue, and gave countless ordinary people their first hands-on experience with diffusion models and voice AI.

**2023-03-10** — **SvcDevelopTeam** open-sourced the initial repository of **So-VITS-SVC** (SoftVC VITS Singing Voice Conversion) on GitHub. Based on VITS[^2] (Variational Inference with adversarial learning for end-to-end TTS) and SoftVC (a soft content encoder), it enabled timbre conversion for singing — preserving the original song's pitch, rhythm, and intonation while replacing the voice with a target singer's timbre. The project provided a GUI and an all-in-one package (including a Colab notebook), significantly lowering the barrier to entry. Licensed under AGPL-3.0.[^1]

**2023-05** — **The "AI Stefanie Sun" phenomenon erupted.** Bilibili users applied SoVITS-SVC to train Stefanie Sun's voice model and used it on a vast number of popular songs — from "Song of a Hero" to Jay Chou's catalogue. The videos went viral, sparking nationwide discussion. A large number of users experienced AI for the first time by downloading the all-in-one package and training voice models. An active voice model sharing community subsequently formed on HuggingFace.[^3]

**Shortly after** — Due to legal risks (copyright and portrait rights disputes), the SoVITS-SVC project announced it would **cease maintenance** (archived). The repository README stated: "This round of time-limited updates is coming to an end; the repository will enter Archive status." GitHub stars had surpassed 28k at the time of archiving (as of 2026-05 retrospection), with 5k+ forks. Community forks and model sharing continued to thrive on Bilibili and HuggingFace.[^1]

### Successor: GPT-SoVITS (January 2024)

**2024-01-14** — **RVC-Boss** created the **GPT-SoVITS** repository on GitHub, combining GPT (Generative Pre-trained Transformer) with SoVITS to achieve **few-shot voice cloning** — "1 min voice data can also be used to train a good TTS model." The project was licensed under MIT.[^4]

GPT-SoVITS quickly became the de facto standard for Chinese open-source voice synthesis. As of May 2026, the repository had accumulated 57.7k stars and 6.3k forks, and was still actively iterating. It preserved the WebUI ease-of-use tradition of SoVITS-SVC while dramatically improving synthesis quality and cross-language capabilities.

### Impact

The SoVITS-SVC → GPT-SoVITS lineage represents one of the **earliest breakout examples of consumer-grade AI applications in the large model era.** Its impact extended far beyond voice synthesis itself:

- **Mass AI experience**: "AI Stefanie Sun" enabled millions of ordinary users to operate AI models with their own hands for the first time — downloading packages, training voice models, generating cover songs — making "AI" a verb even before ChatGPT did.
- **Springboard for technical literacy**: Through tinkering with SoVITS and GPT-SoVITS, large numbers of users encountered concepts like the VITS architecture, diffusion models (DDPM), flow matching, and vocoders for the first time, leading many to enter the field of AI research and development.
- **Model-sharing ecosystem**: A massive voice model sharing community formed on Bilibili and HuggingFace, with thousands of character models trained and publicly shared, giving rise to a unique "AI cover song" subculture.
- **Barrier-lowering paradigm**: From requiring hours of high-quality recording (traditional TTS) to "1 minute of voice is enough," GPT-SoVITS rewrote the accessibility of voice cloning.
