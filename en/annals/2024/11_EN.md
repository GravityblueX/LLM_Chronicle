# November 2024

## DeepSeek-R1-Lite: R1's advance guard

**2024-11-20** — DeepSeek released **DeepSeek-R1-Lite**, launched as a preview of DeepSeek-R1 on the DeepSeek website with open trial access. R1-Lite was DeepSeek's first publicly demonstrated reasoning model, employing a mode similar to o1's "think first, then answer," with strong performance on math, code, and logical reasoning tasks.[^1]

R1-Lite's timing was shrewd. OpenAI's o1-preview had launched two months earlier (2024-09-12), defining the "reasoning model" category. DeepSeek's rapid follow-up with R1-Lite signaled to the world that Chinese teams were in close pursuit in this category. Just over two months later (2025-01-20), DeepSeek-R1 — refined from R1-Lite — was officially released and open-sourced under the MIT license, fundamentally reshaping the reasoning model competitive landscape (see *Annals, January 2025*).

## Qwen2.5-Coder: A new benchmark for open-source code models

**2024-11-12** — The Alibaba Tongyi Qianwen team released **Qwen2.5-Coder**, open-sourcing 1.5B and 7B sizes (a 32B version followed), trained on 5.5T tokens of code data. Qwen2.5-Coder showed strong performance on code benchmarks including HumanEval, MBPP, and LiveCodeBench, with the 7B version surpassing GPT-3.5-Turbo on multiple metrics.[^2]

Qwen2.5-Coder was the code-specialized sub-series of the Qwen2.5 family. Its strategy was "small but exceptional" — not scaling to 70B parameters, but pushing the limits in the coding direction. This paralleled the strategies of Code Llama and DeepSeek-Coder: training a dedicated code model focused on generating, understanding, debugging, and refactoring code.

## Qwen2.5-Turbo: Million-token context

**2024-11-25** — Alibaba Cloud released **Qwen2.5-Turbo**, extending context length from 128K to 1M tokens (approximately 1 million English words or 1.5 million Chinese characters, equivalent to 10 complete novels). It achieved 100% accuracy on the 1M-length Passkey Retrieval task, with a RULER long-text evaluation score of 93.1 — surpassing GPT-4's 91.6 and GLM4-9B-1M's 89.9. Through sparse attention mechanisms, time-to-first-token was reduced from 4.9 minutes to 68 seconds, a 4.3x speedup.[^3]

The practical significance of 1M context was that a model could read an enterprise's entire internal documentation, a complete codebase, or an ultra-long legal contract in a single pass — without requiring an external RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) system. Pricing remained at ¥0.3 per million tokens, approximately 28% the cost of GPT-4o-mini at equivalent context efficiency.

## Mistral Large: European faction upgrade

**2024-11-18** — Mistral AI updated **Mistral Large** (later called Mistral Large 2411) on the le Chat platform, with major enhancements to long-context processing, function calling, and system prompt adherence. The new version narrowed the gap with GPT-4o and Claude 3.5 Sonnet across multiple benchmarks, while maintaining advantages in multilingual tasks (especially European languages like French, German, and Spanish).[^4]

Unlike OpenAI and Anthropic, Mistral's strategy had always been "not pursuing being the biggest, but being the most efficient." Mistral Large continued the MoE route, using sparse activation to maintain relatively low inference costs while sustaining near-frontier capability levels.

## Anthropic's Claude 3.5 Haiku

**Around 2024-11-04** — Anthropic officially released **Claude 3.5 Haiku**, previously announced alongside the upgraded Claude 3.5 Sonnet on 2024-10-22. Claude 3.5 Haiku achieved or surpassed the previous generation's Claude 3 Opus performance across multiple evaluations, with inference speed comparable to the prior Haiku. This was an important step for Anthropic in the "small model, high performance" direction — delivering near-flagship capability at lower cost and faster speed.[^5]

On the same day, Anthropic announced the **computer use** feature entering public beta: developers could have Claude operate a computer screen like a human — moving the cursor, clicking buttons, typing text. This was the first frontier model to offer such capability, marking the transition of large models from "understanding and generating" to "directly manipulating the environment."[^5]

## Other events

**2024-11-05** — Tencent released **Hunyuan-Large**, a 389B-parameter MoE architecture with 52B active parameters. Tencent claimed it achieved frontier open-source model performance on MMLU, CMMLU, C-Eval, and other comprehensive benchmarks, with weights released under a permissive license.[^6]

**2024-11-15** — OpenAI rolled out GPT-4o's **Advanced Voice Mode** global update to ChatGPT Plus users, adding custom instruction support and improved accent recognition. On the same day, ChatGPT mobile monthly active users were reported to have surpassed 300 million.

**2024-11-19** — Microsoft announced **Azure AI Foundry** at its Ignite conference, unifying Azure AI Studio and Azure ML into a single platform for model catalog, evaluation, safety filtering, and deployment. The company also announced an AI agent development framework and additional industry solutions.

**2024-11-28** — Multiple media outlets reported that OpenAI was negotiating a new funding round at a **$150 billion valuation**, making it one of the highest-valued private AI companies globally. Simultaneously, discussions about OpenAI's transition to a for-profit entity intensified.

## Commentary

November 2024 was the month the "reasoning era" of large models was confirmed. o1 pioneered the reasoning model category in September, and DeepSeek-R1-Lite proved in November that this category would not be OpenAI's exclusive territory. Reasoning capability was transforming from a "proprietary secret" into an "admission requirement."

Meanwhile, context length, multimodality, code specialization, and voice interaction were all advancing simultaneously. A common logic ran through all of them: large models needed not just to "get bigger" — they needed to progress on more dimensions at once — reasoning depth, context windows, multimodal fusion, product interaction — each dimension potentially becoming the breakthrough point for the next round of competition.
