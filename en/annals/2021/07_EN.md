# July 2021

## ERNIE 3.0: The 10B Milestone for Chinese Large Models

### Release

**2021-07** — Baidu released **ERNIE 3.0** with 10B parameters, making it the largest Chinese pre-trained model at the time. ERNIE 3.0 integrated autoregressive and autoencoding networks — simultaneously training both language understanding and language generation capabilities within a unified framework.[^1]

ERNIE 3.0 set new state-of-the-art across 54 Chinese NLP benchmarks, spanning tasks including reading comprehension, sentiment analysis, text generation, and knowledge reasoning. The series later iterated to ERNIE 4.0 (2023), which powered Baidu's Ernie Bot (Wenxin Yiyan) product.

### Commentary

The value of the ERNIE series lies not in technical originality — knowledge enhancement, multi-stage pre-training, and similar ideas all had international counterparts — but in ensuring that Chinese NLP had independent infrastructure in the pre-training era. From ERNIE 1.0 (2019, 110M) to 3.0 (2021, 10B), the parameter count grew nearly 100-fold, consistently benchmarked against the world's most advanced models. In the geopolitical competition over large models, the specificity of the Chinese language (vocabulary, semantic structure, cultural knowledge) necessitated independently trained models, rather than relying solely on translation and adaptation.
