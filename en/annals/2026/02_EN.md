# February 2026

## A month of intensive model releases

> February 2026 was a period of intensive model releases — OpenAI, Google, Anthropic, and Alibaba each launched five major models or variants within the same month. The distillation attack allegations (see below) occurred against this backdrop: every player was vying for the technological high ground.

**2026-02-05** — OpenAI released **GPT-5.3-Codex**, positioned as a competitor to Claude Opus 4.6, focusing on code generation, speed, and repository search capabilities. Technical benchmarks showed it was 25% faster than Opus 4.6. OpenAI called it "the first model to participate in creating itself" — meaning GPT-5.3-Codex had used its own predecessor Codex variant during development to assist in writing code. Ars Technica, 2026-02-05[^2]; ZDNET, 2026-02-05[^3]

**2026-02-12** — Google released **Gemini 3 Deep Think**, the deep reasoning variant of the Gemini 3 series, targeting OpenAI's Thinking mode and Anthropic's extended reasoning feature. Wikipedia, "Gemini (chatbot)"[^4]

**2026-02-16** — Alibaba released the **Qwen3.5** series, comprising Qwen3.5 (open-source) and Qwen3.5-Plus. Wikipedia, "Qwen"[^5]

**2026-02-17** — Anthropic released **Claude Sonnet 4.6**, the latest iteration of the Sonnet series. Wikipedia, "Claude (language model)"[^6]

**2026-02-19** — Google released **Gemini 3.1 Pro**, a performance-enhanced variant of the Gemini 3 series. Wikipedia, "Gemini (chatbot)"[^4]

## Distillation attack allegations

**2026-02-23** — Anthropic publicly alleged on its official blog that three Chinese AI companies — **DeepSeek**, **Moonshot AI**, and **MiniMax** — had conducted "industrial-scale model distillation attacks" by using approximately 24,000 fake accounts to conduct over 16 million conversation exchanges with Claude. [^1]

The detailed course of events, reactions from all parties, and the "March 18 Incident" (March 16–18, 2026, involving the collective banning of Chinese users by Anthropic/OpenAI/Google) it triggered are all detailed in *Annals, March 2026*.
