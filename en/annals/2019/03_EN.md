# March 2019

## ERNIE 1.0: The "Pre-training Manifesto" for Chinese NLP

> Baidu released ERNIE 1.0 — China's first large-scale pre-trained language model, surpassing BERT on Chinese NLP benchmarks. This marked the first time the Chinese NLP community possessed pre-training infrastructure on par with the rest of the world.

### Release

**2019-03** — Baidu released **ERNIE 1.0** (Enhanced Representation through kNowledge IntEgration) with 110M parameters, comparable in scale to BERT-base. The paper proposed a "knowledge-enhanced" continual learning paradigm — first training on large-scale Chinese corpora, then progressively incorporating structured knowledge from encyclopedic sources, dialogue data, and other resources for multi-stage pre-training.[^1]

Unlike BERT, which randomly masks individual Chinese characters, ERNIE's masking strategy accounts for Chinese semantic units — entities (such as "Beijing") and phrases are masked as a whole, forcing the model to learn more complete semantic knowledge.

### Impact

ERNIE marked the beginning of Chinese large language models. From that point on, Chinese NLP transitioned from "translating English models + fine-tuning on Chinese" to an era of autonomous pre-training. Baidu iterated the ERNIE series to version 3.0 (2021, 10B) and 4.0 (2023), establishing it as a major force in the Chinese LLM landscape.

### Commentary

The pre-training era in English NLP was heralded by BERT; in Chinese NLP, it was heralded by ERNIE — the two were separated by less than five months. ERNIE 1.0 was essentially a Chinese adaptation of BERT, but its significance lay not in technical originality, but in demonstrating one thing: Chinese NLP did not need to forever follow in English's footsteps through translation and adaptation — it could develop its own pre-trained models and evaluation standards.
