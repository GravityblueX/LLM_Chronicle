## Notes

### DeepSeek / High-Flyer Capital Management

DeepSeek (formally: Hangzhou DeepSeek Artificial Intelligence Basic Technology Research Co., Ltd.) does not follow the traditional VC funding route. Its parent company, **High-Flyer Capital Management**, is a leading Chinese quantitative hedge fund, founded by Liang Wenfeng in 2015, with approximately $10–15B* in assets under management. DeepSeek's computing infrastructure and R&D expenditures are funded by High-Flyer's own capital, and the exact scale of investment is not externally verifiable. Based on public information estimates, DeepSeek's cumulative compute investment from 2023 to 2025 may exceed $1B — but this is an estimate, not a company disclosure.[^27]

### Valuation methodology notes

- OpenAI's early 2023 $29B valuation was a post-money valuation following Microsoft's investment, but OpenAI at the time operated under a "capped-profit" structure with investment returns capped at 100× the investment amount.[^3]
- Anthropic's Amazon investment used a convertible note structure and was not priced as a traditional VC round.[^11]
- The valuation surges of some companies in 2024–2025 (OpenAI $157B→$300B, Anthropic $18.4B→$61.5B, xAI $24B→$50B) reflect the pace of AI industry valuation inflation — but these valuations are determined by highly illiquid private markets, which operate differently from public market pricing mechanisms.
- All numbers marked with `*` are media estimates, not official confirmations or regulatory disclosures.

### Scope of coverage

This table only includes **funding events with public, traceable reports**. Funding rounds with undisclosed amounts (e.g., some Chinese companies' Series A/B) are not included. Strategic investments (e.g., Microsoft's investment in OpenAI, Amazon's investment in Anthropic) are separately labeled as "Strategic investment."
